# Commit plan

Twelve commits grouped into four chunks, one chunk per working day. Each chunk
leaves the repository in a state that runs and that tells a coherent story to
anyone reading the history: scaffolding, then baselines, then the experiment,
then the write-up. Nothing in a later chunk is needed to make an earlier one work.

Run a chunk with:

```bash
./scripts/commit_day.sh 1
```

`./scripts/commit_day.sh --list` prints every commit without touching git, and
`--all` does all four chunks at once if you would rather not spread them out.

The script sets `GIT_AUTHOR_DATE` and `GIT_COMMITTER_DATE` to the moment you run
it, so committing on four separate days gives you four separate days of history.

---

## Day 1 — scaffolding and data

Gets the repo standing up and both datasets loading. Nothing here is modelling.

1. **`chore: project scaffolding, pinned deps and Makefile`**
   `.gitignore`, `LICENSE`, `requirements.txt`, `Makefile`, `src/lown/__init__.py`, `src/lown/config.py`, `scripts/README.md`
2. **`feat(data): TDC loaders for SAbDab_Chen and TAP`**
   `src/lown/data.py`
   TDC returns both datasets as a stringified sequence container, and the two
   datasets disagree about which container. Parsed with a regex over amino-acid runs.
3. **`feat(embeddings): cache frozen ESM-2 650M chain embeddings`**
   `src/lown/embeddings.py`, `scripts/02_embed.py`
   Forward pass only. Chains are deduplicated across both datasets first, which
   takes 5,300 chain slots down to 3,238 unique sequences.

## Day 2 — the baselines you have to beat

The part that makes the benchmark credible. No language model in sight.

4. **`feat(features): amino-acid composition and Biopython descriptors`**
   `src/lown/features.py`
5. **`feat(heads): linear, MLP and gradient-boosting heads with inner-CV tuning`**
   `src/lown/heads.py`
   Includes the fallback for subsamples that land with a single positive
   antibody, which is a real failure mode at N=25 and a 20% positive rate.
6. **`exp(phase2): audit the cheap baselines and report their coefficients`**
   `scripts/03_baselines.py`, `results/baseline_scores.csv`, `results/baseline_coefficients.csv`

## Day 3 — splits and the sweep

The experiment itself, and the honest-split machinery that makes it worth reading.

7. **`feat(splits): identity clustering and cluster-held-out partitions`**
   `src/lown/splits.py`
8. **`exp(phase0): dataset, clustering and leakage summaries`**
   `scripts/01_prepare_data.py`, `results/dataset_summary.csv`,
   `results/cdr_extraction_check.csv`, `results/cluster_summary.csv`, `results/leakage_summary.csv`
   Two thirds of a random SAbDab test set has a near-duplicate in training.
9. **`feat(experiment): nested-subsample learning-curve sweep`**
   `src/lown/experiment.py`, `scripts/04_learning_curves.py`
10. **`exp(phase3): full sweep results`**
    `results/learning_curves.csv`

## Day 4 — figures and the write-up

11. **`feat(plots): learning-curve, split-gap and sample-efficiency figures`**
    `src/lown/plots.py`, `src/lown/scaling.py`, `scripts/06_figures.py`, `figures/`, the derived tables in `results/`
    Includes the saturating power-law fit that turns a learning curve into "how
    many more antibodies would it take".
12. **`docs: README with learning curves and findings`**
    `README.md`, `COMMIT_PLAN.md`, `notebooks/analysis.ipynb`
